# Croakitori V1.0 Release Notes
## Initial release of Croakitori, a game where food is illegal, and you do crimes.

### Platforms Supported:
- Windows 10/11

### Features:
  - Explore the underground world of food in Taiwan using your tongue.
  - Cook extravagant dishes to impress your father and gain respect in the culinary world.
  - Seek your father's approval and prove that Chinese cuisine is worth celebrating.

### See the README.md or itch.io page for controls, roadmap, credits, etc.

### Known Issues:
- Mysterious glitch in the platforming section where on death the screen occasionally stays black and the player is unable to respawn (if you have ANY information on this please contact will.kuntze@arborium.dev)
